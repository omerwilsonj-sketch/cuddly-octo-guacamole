# FluentPath Spanish — YouTube Advertisement Strategy

## Overview
YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine and reaches over 2.5 billion logged-in users monthly. This strategy leverages both **paid YouTube Ads (Google Ads)** and **organic/free distribution** to put FluentPath Spanish in front of a global audience — not just people actively searching for Spanish lessons, but professionals, expats, and curious learners across the entire platform.

---

## 1. YouTube Ad Formats We'll Use

| Format | Duration | When to Use | Cost Model |

| **Skippable In-Stream** | 15–60 sec | Primary driver. Run before/after/mid video. | CPV (pay only if watched 30 sec or interacted) |
| **Bumper Ads** | 6 sec | Brand recall, retargeting, quick hooks. | CPM |

> **Rule of Thumb:** Skippable In-Stream + Shorts for cold audiences. Bumpers for retargeting. In-Feed for high-intent keywords.

## 3. Visual Production Guide

### Brand Elements to Include in Every Ad
1. **Logo:** `fluentpath-logo-new.svg` (bottom right or center at end card)
2. **Primary Color:** Deep Teal `#1A5F7A` — backgrounds, text boxes, lower thirds
3. **Accent Color:** Golden Yellow `#F2BE22` — CTAs, badges, highlights
4. **Secondary Color:** Terracotta `#C45B28` — emotional moments, relationship/culture angles
5. **Typography:** Montserrat for headlines, Inter for body/on-screen text

### Filming Tips (Low-Budget / High-Impact)
- **Hook in 0–5 seconds:** The skip button appears at 5 seconds. Start with motion, a bold question, or a relatable face.
- **Faces win:** Close-ups of human faces outperform animated explainer videos on YouTube.
- **Captions are mandatory:** 85% of Facebook videos are watched without sound. On YouTube, many viewers keep sound off until engaged. Burn in large, readable captions.
- **Vertical + Horizontal:** Film horizontal (16:9) for In-Stream and In-Feed. Crop vertical (9:16) for Shorts.
- **End Card (last 5 sec):** Always end with logo, URL, and a single CTA. No distractions.

### Music & Sound
- **In-Stream:** Start silent or ambient → build to upbeat, modern Latin instrumental (no lyrics to distract).
- **Shorts:** Use trending audio where possible (for organic) or punchy royalty-free beats (for paid).
- **Voiceover:** Warm, confident, slightly British or neutral North American accent. Avoid overly salesy tone.

## 5. Free / Organic YouTube Strategy

YouTube's algorithm rewards watch time, consistency, and click-through rate. A strong organic channel compounds over time and feeds the paid funnel with warm audiences.

### Channel Setup
- **Channel Name:** FluentPath Spanish
- **Profile:** Use `fluentpath-logo-new.png` as profile picture. Banner with tagline: "Real Spanish. Real Conversations. Real Fluency."
- **About Section:** SEO-optimized description with keywords: "Spanish tutoring," "business Spanish," "conversational fluency," "dialect coaching," "learn Spanish for work."
- **Links:** Link to website, free assessment, booking page, and subscription plan.
- **Playlists:** Organize by dialect and topic.

### Content Pillars (Weekly Uploads)

| Pillar | Format | Example Titles | Goal |
|--------|--------|----------------|------|
| **Micro-Lessons** | Shorts (60 sec) | "The ONE phrase you need in a Mexican office" | Viral reach, brand awareness |
| **Dialect Deep-Dives** | Long-form (8–12 min) | "Mexican vs. Castilian: 5 Words That Will Make or Break Your Meeting" | Authority, SEO, lead gen |
| **Student Stories** | Testimonial (3–5 min) | "How Mark Closed a $2M Deal in Chilean Spanish" | Social proof, conversion |
| **Real-World Scenarios** | Roleplay (5–8 min) | "Booking an Apartment in Barcelona: Full Conversation Breakdown" | Practical value, shares |
| **Myth-Busting** | Reaction / Commentary (6–10 min) | "Why Duolingo Will Never Make You Fluent" | Controversy, comments, algorithm boost |

### SEO Tactics
1. **Keyword Research:** Use YouTube's search suggest + tools like TubeBuddy (free tier) to find high-volume, low-competition keywords.
2. **Titles:** Front-load keywords. Use numbers and emotional triggers. E.g., "5 Business Spanish Phrases That Impress LATAM Clients (With Pronunciation)"
3. **Thumbnails:** High contrast. Face + big text. Brand color accents (Teal #1A5F7A + Yellow #F2BE22). Consistent style across all videos.
4. **Descriptions:** First 2 lines = hook + CTA. Include timestamps, links to free session, and keyword-rich paragraphs.
5. **Tags:** Mix broad ("learn Spanish"), niche ("business Spanish Mexico"), and competitor tags.
6. **End Screens:** Last 20 seconds = subscribe button + link to most relevant video + link to website free trial.
7. **Cards:** Add info cards at 30% and 60% mark linking to assessment or booking page.

### Collaboration Strategy
- **Micro-Influencers (10k–100k subs):** Partner with travel vloggers, digital nomad creators, and language learning YouTubers for guest appearances or sponsored segments.
- **Cross-Promotion:** Offer free coaching sessions to YouTubers in exchange for an honest review or "learning Spanish for 30 days" challenge video.
- **Podcast Guests:** Appear on expat podcasts, remote work shows, and language learning interviews. Repurpose clips as YouTube Shorts.

### Community Tab & Shorts
- **Community Tab:** Polls ("Which dialect should we cover next?"), behind-the-scenes coaching clips, quick tips.
- **Shorts:** Post 3–5 Shorts per week. They are the fastest way to reach new audiences on YouTube today. Repurpose TikTok/Reels content if available.

## 7. Success Metrics & Optimization

### KPIs to Track Weekly
1. **View Rate** (In-Stream): % of viewers who watch past 30 sec. Target: >15% for cold, >25% for retargeting.
2. **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Target: >1% for In-Stream, >3% for In-Feed.
3. **Cost Per View (CPV):** Target: <$0.12 for cold, <$0.06 for retargeting.
4. **Cost Per Click (CPC):** Target: <$1.50 for In-Feed.
5. **Free Trial Sign-Ups:** Ultimate conversion metric.
6. **Trial-to-Paid Rate:** Measures quality of traffic.
7. **Organic Subscribers Gained:** Measure channel growth from ad-driven awareness.

### Optimization Playbook
- **Week 1–2:** Launch all 4 cold ad groups with equal budget. Kill any ad with <10% view rate or >$0.20 CPV.
- **Week 3–4:** Shift 70% of budget to the top 2 performing ad groups. Introduce In-Feed for those segments.
- **Month 2:** Launch retargeting campaigns for website visitors and video engagers. A/B test bumper vs. 15-sec skippable.
- **Month 3+:** Scale winning creatives by 20% per week. Introduce new hooks based on top-performing organic Shorts.
