3 Mistakes That Scream

YOU'RE A FOREIGNER

Fix these and sound local instantly.

1
"Estoy embarazada"

You just said you're pregnant — not embarrassed.
Say "Tengo vergüenza" instead.

2
"Soy caliente"

You said you're horny — not hot (temperature).
Say "Tengo calor".

3
"Bizarro" ≠ Bizarre

In Spanish, bizarro means brave or dashing.
For "bizarre," say "extraño" or "raro".
