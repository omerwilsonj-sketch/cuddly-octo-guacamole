# FluentPath Spanish: Revenue Projections (GBP)

This document provides financial forecasts for FluentPath Spanish, incorporating the "Free to Start" business model and the YouTube Ads acquisition strategy.

## 1. Product Definitions & Financial Model
To ensure financial sustainability, the products are defined as follows for this model:

*   **Subscription Plan (£19/mo introductory):** Includes **unlimited access to Small Group Conversation Classes** (4-6 students) and digital lesson materials. 
    *   *Note:* This is a high-value hook for committed learners. 1-on-1 sessions are NOT included.
*   **1-on-1 Coaching (£60/hr avg):** Premium, personalized instruction. Billed per session or in packages.
*   **Single Group Session (£27.50 avg):** Pay-as-you-go option for students who do not want a subscription.

## 2. Model Assumptions (Monthly)
*   **Ad Spend:** £3,240 ($4,050 USD)
*   **Total New Trials:** 30 (Paid Ads) + 5 (Organic/Referral) = 35 total.
*   **Trial-to-Subscription:** 25% (~9 new subs/mo)
*   **Trial-to-Coaching:** 20% (~7 new coaching clients/mo)
*   **Trial-to-Group (One-off):** 10% (~3.5 new one-off group students/mo)
