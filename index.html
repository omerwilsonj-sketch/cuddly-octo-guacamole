Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be fluent to start?

No, but our coaching is designed for intermediate-to-advanced learners. If you know the basics but struggle to use them in real conversation, we're the perfect fit.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

When you sign up for our subscription plan, your first 7 days are completely free. You can attend unlimited sessions and cancel anytime before the trial ends without being charged.

Can I switch between dialects?

Yes! If your needs change, you can let your coach know, and we will adjust the focus of your sessions to the specific dialect you require.
