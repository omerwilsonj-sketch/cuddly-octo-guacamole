# FluentPath Spanish - Brand Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Vibe
Professional, Warm, Fluency-focused, Practical, and Trustworthy.
Geared towards professionals, expats, and adults aiming for real-world fluency in Latin American markets.

## Color Palette
The color system balances professional trustworthiness with the warmth of conversational learning and Latin American culture.

- **Primary (Deep Teal):** `#0F4C5C` 
  - Professional, grounded, trustworthy. Used for main headings, primary buttons, and navbar.
- **Secondary (Terracotta):** `#E36414` 
  - Warm, energetic, conversational. Used for accents, secondary buttons, and highlights.
- **Accent (Golden Yellow):** `#F4A261` 
  - Bright, encouraging, representing fluency and progress. Used for badges, subtle accents, or illustrations.
- **Neutral Background (Off-White):** `#FAFAFA` 
  - Clean, modern background.
- **Text (Slate/Charcoal):** `#2B2D42` 
  - High contrast for readability without being stark black.

## Typography
Clean, modern, and highly legible across devices.

- **Headings:** `Montserrat`
  - A geometric sans-serif that looks professional yet approachable. 
  - *Weights:* Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700).
- **Body:** `Inter` (or `Open Sans`)
  - A versatile, highly legible sans-serif for UI elements, lesson materials, and long-form text.
  - *Weights:* Regular (400), Medium (500).
