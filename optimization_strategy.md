# FluentPath Spanish - Campaign Optimization Strategy (Next 14 Days)
## Managing and Optimizing Meta & Google Ads Campaigns

Now that the initial Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns are live, the focus shifts to data collection, A/B testing, and bid optimization to ensure maximum ROI and a low Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

### 1. A/B Testing Strategy (Meta Ads)
**Phase 1 (Days 1-7): Creative vs. Creative**
- **Hypothesis:** Does the brand-centric photography (e.g., `fluentpath_expat_ad_v2.png`) outperform user-generated content (UGC) style videos?
- **Action:** We will introduce a new UGC-style video ad into the existing ad sets. We will monitor the Click-Through Rate (CTR) and Cost Per Click (CPC).
- **Success Metric:** Winner declared based on the lowest Cost Per Landing Page View.

**Phase 2 (Days 8-14): Copy vs. Copy**
- **Hypothesis:** Does emphasizing "No Grammar Drills" drive more clicks than emphasizing "Speak with Confidence Before You Move"?
- **Action:** For the winning creative from Phase 1, duplicate the ad and swap the primary text and headlines.
- **Success Metric:** Winner declared based on the highest CTR and highest conversion rate on the landing page.

### 2. Bid Adjustment Strategy (Google Ads)
**Days 1-7: Maximize Clicks (Data Gathering)**
- The current bid strategy is "Maximize Clicks" to drive traffic and feed data into the Google Ads algorithm.
- **Daily Action:** Monitor Search Terms Report to add negative keywords (e.g., "free spanish lessons", "spanish grammar book"). This prevents wasted spend.

**Days 8-14: Transition to Target CPA (tCPA)**
- Once the campaigns achieve at least 15 conversions (form fills or trial bookings), we will switch the bid strategy from "Maximize Clicks" to "Target CPA".
- **Action:** Set the initial Target CPA slightly higher (10-15%) than the actual CPA achieved in week 1 to give the algorithm room to breathe, then slowly step it down every 3 days.

### 3. Audience & Placement Optimization
**Meta Ads Optimization:**
- **Placement Review:** After 7 days, review the breakdown by placement. If the Audience Network is driving cheap clicks but zero conversions, exclude it.
- **Demographic Review:** Analyze performance by age group. If the 45-55 age bracket is converting at half the cost of the 28-35 bracket, reallocate budget to the older demographic.

**Google Ads Optimization:**
- **Location Bidding:** After 14 days, apply bid modifiers to top-performing locations. (e.g., +10% bid adjustment for users searching from Austin, TX or Miami, FL if they show higher conversion rates).
- **Device Bidding:** Adjust bids based on mobile vs. desktop performance (e.g., -20% on mobile if mobile traffic bounces quickly).

### Next Steps & Reporting:
A comprehensive performance report will be generated on Day 14 detailing the winning creatives, final CPA, and recommendations for scaling the budget.
