The Word

"You"

Three countries.
Three completely different rules.

🇦🇷

Argentina

Vos

"Vos sos"

"Tú" doesn't exist here.
They use vos with different verb endings.
Sos, tenés, podés, querés.

🇲🇽

Mexico

Tú

"Tú eres"

Standard "tú" like you learned.
But "ustedes" for everyone — boss,
friends, mom. No vosotros. 😮‍💨

🇪🇸

Spain

Tú

+ Vosotros

"Tú" for one person, yes.
But "vosotros" for you-all (friends).
"Ustedes" only if you're being formal. 🤵

The Point

Imagine learning Spanish in Mexico,
then moving to Buenos Aires.

"Tú eres" → blank stares.
