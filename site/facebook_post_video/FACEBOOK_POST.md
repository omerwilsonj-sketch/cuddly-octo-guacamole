## 1. THE VIDEO

**File:** `/home/team/shared/facebook_post_video/reel_you_dialect.html`
**Format:** Self-contained HTML animation — open in browser, screen-record

### What it is
A ~18-second looping vertical video (9:16, 540×960) showing how the Spanish word for "you" changes completely across Argentina (vos), Mexico (tú), and Spain (tú + vosotros). Each country gets its own scene with the flag, the word, and a quick breakdown. Ends with a "Free First Session →" CTA.

**Brand palette:** Midnight Fluency — dark #0D0D12 background, #C8963E gold accents, muted grey text.

### Audio (Spanish TTS, dialect-correct)
- 🇦🇷 "Vos sos" / "tenés" — spoken in **es-AR** (Argentine Spanish voice)
- 🇲🇽 "Tú eres" / "ustedes" — spoken in **es-MX** (Mexican Spanish voice)
- 🇪🇸 "Tú" / "vosotros" / "ustedes" — spoken in **es-ES** (Castilian Spanish voice)

### Hook (first 1-2 seconds on-screen text):
**"The Word 'You'"** — bold gold type animating in, followed by "Three countries. Three completely different rules."

## 2. HOW TO RECORD & POST

### Step 1: Screen-record the video
1. Open `reel_you_dialect.html` in Chrome (double-click the file, or right-click → Open With → Chrome)
2. Click "🔊 Tap for sound" to enable Spanish audio
3. Use QuickTime (Mac) or OBS / Xbox Game Bar (Windows) to screen-record the browser window
4. Crop to the 540×960 reel area (9:16 vertical)
5. Trim to 18 seconds

**Alternatively:** Open the file on your phone (transfer or host locally), then use your phone's built-in screen recorder.

### Step 2: Post to Facebook
1. Upload the video as a **Facebook Reel** (or regular feed video — 9:16 works in both)
2. Copy and paste the caption below
3. Paste the hashtags after the caption
