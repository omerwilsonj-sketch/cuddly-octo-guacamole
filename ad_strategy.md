# FluentPath Spanish - Ad Strategy & Creative Campaigns

## 1. Audience Targeting Recommendations

**Audience 1: Professionals Relocating & Expats (The "Move" Audience)**
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):**
  - **Behaviors:** Expats (Mexico, Spain, Colombia, etc.), Recently Moved.
  - **Interests:** Travel, International Business, Relocation Services, Specific countries (e.g., Mexico, Costa Rica, Spain).
  - **Demographics:** Age 28-55. Currently living in or frequently traveling to LATAM/Spain.
- **Google Search:**
  - **Keywords:** "Spanish classes for expats", "Learn Spanish before moving to Mexico", "Spanish tutor relocation", "Fast Spanish fluency".

**Audience 2: Love & Relationships (The "Connection" Audience)**
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):**
  - **Interests:** Spanish language, Latin culture, long-distance relationships, bilingual families.
  - **Demographics:** Age 25-45.
- **Google Search:**
  - **Keywords:** "Learn Spanish for my partner", "Spanish lessons for couples", "Conversational Spanish fast".

**Audience 3: LATAM Business Developers & Remote Workers (The "Career" Audience)**
- **LinkedIn Ads (Bonus) / Meta B2B Targeting:**
  - **Job Titles:** Business Development Manager, Sales Director, Remote Worker, Digital Nomad.
  - **Interests:** Latin America Economy, International Trade, Digital Nomad.
- **Google Search:**
  - **Keywords:** "Business Spanish tutoring", "Professional Spanish courses online", "Spanish for work in LATAM".

## 2. Suggested Campaign Structure

**Meta/Instagram (Funnel Approach):**
- **Top of Funnel (Awareness/Prospecting):**
  - **Objective:** Traffic / Conversions (Lead generation).
  - **Ad Sets:** Broken down by Audience (Expats, Relationships, Business).
  - **Creative:** Video testimonials, relatable "language barrier" scenarios, carousel of practical phrases vs grammar drills. Focus heavily on the "Free to Start" hook to lower entry barriers.
- **Middle of Funnel (Retargeting):**
  - **Objective:** Conversions (Booking a session).
  - **Ad Sets:** Website visitors (last 30 days), Instagram/FB page engagers.
  - **Creative:** Special "Free to Start" offer: First session is free, and the £19/mo subscription features a 7-day free trial. "Why FluentPath?" comparison (Us vs Apps like Duolingo).

**Google Ads (Search Intent Approach):**
- **Campaign 1: Relocation/Expat Intent**
  - Ad Groups: Moving to Mexico, Moving to Spain, Expat Spanish.
- **Campaign 2: Professional/Business Intent**
  - Ad Groups: Business Spanish, Corporate Spanish tutoring, Spanish for work.
- **Campaign 3: Relationship Intent**
  - Ad Groups: Spanish for partner, conversational Spanish fast.
