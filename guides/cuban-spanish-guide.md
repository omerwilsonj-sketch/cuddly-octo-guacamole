# Cuban Spanish Study Guide

¡Oye asere! Welcome to the world of Cuban Spanish. Known for its rapid pace and rhythmic musicality, Cuban Spanish is as unique as the island itself.

## Key Vocabulary
1. **Asere:** Friend/Homie (very common, informal).
2. **Guagua:** Bus (the primary mode of transport).
3. **Máquina:** Old American car (often used as shared taxis).
4. **Jamar:** To eat (slang).
5. **Fajarse:** To fight/argue.
6. **Jeva/Jevo:** Girlfriend/Boyfriend.
7. **Yuma:** A foreigner (especially from the US) or the US itself.
8. **Pulóver:** T-shirt.
9. **Sorbeto:** Drinking straw.
10. **Radio:** Gossip (e.g., "soltar el radio").
11. **Pila:** A lot/A bunch (e.g., "pila de gente").
12. **Papelazo:** An embarrassing situation.
13. **Chola:** Flip-flop/Sandal.
14. **Gacho:** Bad/Ugly (less common than in Mexico, but used).
15. **Dale:** Go ahead / Okay / Bye (the ultimate filler word).

## 10 Common Phrases
1. **¿Qué bolá?:** What’s up? / How’s it going?
2. **¡Oye!:** Hey! / Listen!
3. **¡Está volao!:** It’s amazing / Out of this world!
4. **¡No me digas!:** You don't say! / Really?
5. **¡Echa para allá!:** Get out of here! / No way!
6. **¡Qué clase de...!:** What a...! (e.g., "¡Qué clase de calor!").
7. **¡Vaya!:** Well then! / Wow!
8. **¡Tranquilo!:** No worries / It's all good.
9. **Acere qué bolá:** Hey friend, what's up?
10. **Nos piñamos:** We'll see each other later.

## Grammar Tips
*   **S-Dropping:** Cubans often drop the 's' at the end of syllables. "Gracias" sounds like "Gracia," and "Estamos" sounds like "Etamo."
*   **Consonant Aspiration:** The 'r' at the end of words is often aspirated or swapped with 'l' (though less than in Puerto Rico).
*   **Directness:** Cubans often skip the "usted" and go straight to "tú," even with people they don't know well.
*   **Sentence Rhythm:** The speech is very staccato and rhythmic, often described as "sing-songy."

## Cultural Notes
*   **Coffee Culture:** A "cafecito" (strong, sweet espresso) is the social glue of Cuba. Offering one to a guest is mandatory.
*   **Directness:** Cubans are known for being very direct and vocal. Don't be offended if someone calls you "gordo" or "flaco"—it's often used as a nickname.
*   **Music:** The language is heavily influenced by the rhythms of Son and Salsa. Expect a lot of body language.

## Practice Dialogue
**Pedro:** ¡Asere, qué bolá! ¿Cómo va todo?
**Yuni:** ¡Tranquilo, asere! Aquí esperando la guagua hace una pila de tiempo.
**Pedro:** ¡Qué clase de calor hay hoy! ¿No quieres jamar algo por ahí?
**Yuni:** Dale, me muero de hambre. Pero tengo que cuidar la máquina, que me está dando problemas.
**Pedro:** No te preocupes, vamos en la mía. ¡Está volao el nuevo restaurante de la esquina!
