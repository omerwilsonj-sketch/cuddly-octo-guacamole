# Colombian Spanish Study Guide

¡Bienvenidos a Colombia! Often cited as having one of the clearest and most beautiful accents in the Spanish-speaking world, Colombian Spanish is a delight to learn.

## Key Vocabulary
1. **Parce/Parcero:** Friend/Mate (Iconic to Medellín/Antioquia).
2. **Bacán/Bacano:** Cool / Nice.
3. **Chévere:** Cool / Great.
4. **Plata:** Money.
5. **Rumba:** Party.
6. **Vaina:** Thing (Used for almost anything).
7. **Tinto:** A small black coffee (very common).
8. **Mono/Mona:** Blonde or light-skinned person.
9. **Camellar:** To work hard.
10. **Guayabo:** Hangover.
11. **Jincho:** Drunk.
12. **Traga:** A crush (e.g., "Tener una traga").
13. **Desparchado:** Bored / With nothing to do.
14. **Mañé:** Tacky / Lacking style.
15. **Pilas:** Watch out / Be alert.

## 10 Common Phrases
1. **¿Qué más?:** What’s up? / How are you?
2. **¡Hágale!:** Do it! / Go ahead! / Okay!
3. **¡Qué pena!:** I'm so sorry! (Used for even minor inconveniences).
4. **Con mucho gusto:** You're very welcome (very polite).
5. **¡Qué nota!:** How cool! / That's great!
6. **Estar amañado:** To feel at home / To be happy in a place.
7. **¡Qué hubo! (¡Q'hubo!):** What's up!
8. **No dar papaya:** Don't put yourself in a vulnerable position / Don't tempt fate.
9. **Me hace un favor:** Can you do me a favor? (Standard polite request).
10. **¿Cierto?:** Right? (Used frequently at the end of sentences).

## Grammar Tips
*   **Use of "Usted":** In many parts of Colombia (especially Bogotá and the Andean region), "Usted" is used even between close friends and family members. It’s a sign of affection and respect.
*   **Diminutives with "-ico":** Instead of "-ito," you’ll often hear "-ico" if the word ends in 't'. *Gato* becomes *gatico*, and *momento* becomes *momentico*.
*   **Clear Pronunciation:** Colombians (especially from the interior) tend to pronounce every letter clearly, making it an excellent dialect for learners.
*   **"Ustedes":** Like all of LATAM, "vosotros" is never used.

## Cultural Notes
*   **Politeness:** Colombians are exceptionally polite. "A la orden" (at your service) and "Con mucho gusto" are phrases you will hear multiple times a day.
*   **Coffee Culture:** Coffee is part of the identity. A "tinto" is the standard social drink.
*   **Regional Diversity:** There is a big difference between a "Rolo" (from Bogotá), a "Paisa" (from Medellín), and a "Costeño" (from the coast).

## Practice Dialogue
**Elena:** ¡Q'hubo, parce! ¿Qué más?
**Mateo:** Todo bien, Elena. Aquí camellando duro para la rumba del sábado.
**Elena:** ¡Hágale! Va a estar bacano. ¿Ya tienes la plata para la entrada?
**Mateo:** Sí, pero qué pena, no he tenido tiempo de comprarla. ¿Me haces un favor y me la compras tú?
**Elena:** ¡De una! No des papaya que se agotan rápido. ¡Pilas pues!
