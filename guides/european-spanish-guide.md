# European Peninsular Spanish Study Guide

## Key Vocabulary
- Coche (car)
- Piso (apartment/flat)
- Zumo (juice)
- Ordenador (computer)
- Móvil (cell phone)

## Common Phrases
- ¡Vale! — Okay!/Alright!
- ¡Qué guay! — How cool!
- Tengo prisa — I'm in a hurry
- ¿Quieres quedar? — Do you want to meet up?
- De nada — You're welcome

## Grammar Tips
- **Vosotros**: Used as informal "you all" (not used in LATAM)
- **Distinción**: "c/z" pronounced as /θ/ (th), not /s/
- **Leísmo**: "le" used for masculine direct object ("le vi" = "I saw him")

## Cultural Notes
- Lunch is the main meal (2-3pm); dinner is late (9-10pm)
- "Tú" is used more broadly than in many LATAM countries
- Regional languages include Catalan, Basque, and Galician
