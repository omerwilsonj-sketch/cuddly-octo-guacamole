# Chilean Spanish Study Guide

Welcome to Chile! Chilean Spanish is often considered one of the most challenging dialects for learners due to its speed, unique vocabulary, and the "Chilenismos" that color every sentence.

## Key Vocabulary
1. **Al tiro:** Right away / Immediately.
2. **Bacán:** Cool / Awesome.
3. **Cachar:** To understand / To get it (e.g., "¿Cachái?").
4. **Cuático:** Crazy / Intense / Strange.
5. **Fome:** Boring.
6. **Pololo/Polola:** Boyfriend/Girlfriend.
7. **Pega:** Work/Job.
8. **Po:** Filler word (derived from "pues"), used at the end of sentences (¡Sí po!).
9. **Luca:** 1,000 pesos (e.g., "dos lucas").
10. **Taco:** Traffic jam.
11. **Weá:** Thing / Situation (very informal, can be vulgar).
12. **Weón:** Dude / Friend / Idiot (highly context-dependent, use with caution).
13. **Gallada:** Group of people / Folks.
14. **Guagua:** Baby (Note: different from Cuban "bus").
15. **Copuchear:** To gossip.

## 10 Common Phrases
1. **¿Cachái?:** Do you get it? / Do you know?
2. **¡Qué fome!:** How boring!
3. **¡Al tiro!:** Right now!
4. **¡Pucha!:** Shoot! / Darn!
5. **¡Ya po!:** Come on! / Hurry up! / Yes!
6. **¡Estamos listos!:** We're ready!
7. **¡Qué bacán!:** How cool!
8. **Pasarlo chancho:** To have a great time.
9. **Hacer una vaca:** To pool money together (for a party, etc.).
10. **Dar jugo:** To be unproductive / To talk nonsense.

## Grammar Tips
*   **The "-ai" Ending:** Chileans often use a unique second-person ending for informal speech: "¿Cómo estái?" instead of "¿Cómo estás?" or "¿Qué pensái?" instead of "¿Qué piensas?".
*   **Rapid Speech:** Words are often shortened, and the final 's' is frequently dropped or aspirated.
*   **Frequent "Po":** You will hear "Sí po," "No po," and "Ya po" constantly. It serves as punctuation.
*   **Voseo:** Like Argentina, Chile uses a form of "vos," but the conjugation is slightly different and often mixed with "tú."

## Cultural Notes
*   **La Once:** Instead of a heavy dinner, Chileans have "La Once"—a late afternoon tea/coffee with bread (marraqueta or hallulla), avocado (palta), and cheese.
*   **Palta:** Avocado is a staple of the Chilean diet and is called "palta," never "aguacate."
*   **Resilience:** Chilean slang reflects a culture accustomed to natural challenges (like earthquakes).

## Practice Dialogue
**Javi:** ¡Hola po! ¿Cómo estái? ¿Llegaste al tiro?
**Nico:** No po, me pilló un taco cuático. Media hora ahí parado.
**Javi:** ¡Qué fome! Oye, ¿cachái que hoy vamos a hacer una vaca para el asado?
**Nico:** ¡Bacán! Pasémoslo chancho. ¿Cuántas lucas hay que poner?
**Javi:** Con unas cinco lucas estamos listos. ¡Ya po, muévete!
